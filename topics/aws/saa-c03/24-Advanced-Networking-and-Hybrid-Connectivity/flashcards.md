# Flashcards - Advanced Networking and Hybrid Connectivity

1. Peering and transitive?
- No.

2. Routing hub for many VPCs?
- Transit Gateway.

3. Private access to service between accounts without opening the entire network?
- PrivateLink.

4. IPSec connection over the internet?
- Site-to-Site VPN.

5. Dedicated AWS-on-premises link?
- Direct Connect.

6. Best for few VPCs and simple topology?
- VPC Peering.

7. NACL and stateful?
- No, it is stateless.

8. Security Group and stateful?
- Yes.

9. Hybrid DNS for private zone on AWS?
- Route 53 Resolver endpoint.

10. Common backup for DX?
- VPN.
